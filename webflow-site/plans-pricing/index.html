Plan FAQ

Before you choose.

Are provider costs included?
The listed plan allowances describe the coordinated visits included in each plan. Hospital, diagnostic, medicine, and other third-party charges may be separate. Our team will explain the current terms before you enrol.
Can we change plans later?
Yes. Care needs change. Your family can review the level of support with the care team and move to a more suitable plan subject to current availability and terms.
What if my parent is outside a current service city?
Contact us with the postcode. We will confirm whether Anara can provide the required in-person support before recommending a plan.
Is this health insurance?
No. Anara is a care coordination and service plan. It does not replace health insurance or guarantee coverage of hospital and medical costs.
