About Anara India · Concierge Care · est. 2024

India runs on its corporates. Corporates run on their people. Workforce healthcare should act like it.

The thesis

Most workforce health programmes in India measure activity — drives run, mailers sent, vouchers issued. None of those numbers add up to a healthier employee.

Anara was built on a different bet: if the care comes to where work happens, in the format people already use, the outcomes follow. On-site clinics. WhatsApp as a communication channel. India's existing health stack. No new app, no portal, no chasing.

In practice

  1. 01
    The care comes to the office. Doctors, equipment, lab logistics — we set up. Employees show up.
  2. 02
    A real human, every time. A named care manager per employee. No call-centre handoffs, no chatbot triage.
  3. 03
    WhatsApp-first follow-up. Where India already is. A personalised plan within 48 hours of every check.

FAQ

What HR & procurement ask first.

Will this add work for our HR team?
Your HR lead confirms the date and shares the employee list. We handle equipment, doctors, reminders, lab coordination, and the post-clinic report. Most HR contacts tell us we're the one healthcare programme they don't have to manage.
What participation rate can we realistically expect?
85%+ across client sites. We come during working hours, to your office, with everything set up. Industry average for externally-run programmes is 40–50%.
Can you cover our employees' parents?
Yes — Care Managers do weekly home visits across our service cities, plus on-demand telehealth. They manage medications, lab coordination, hospital accompaniment, and send real-time WhatsApp updates to the employee.
What does executive health look like for senior leadership?
A dedicated session at your office — cardiovascular risk profiling, executive stress assessment, full blood panel, 1:1 consultation. No waiting. Full confidentiality. Leaders get a personal summary and a 12-month preventive plan.
Will Anara interfere with our existing TPA or broker?
No. Anara works alongside your GMC and TPA, not instead of them. Your broker relationship doesn't change.
