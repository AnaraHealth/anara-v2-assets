our mission

Assisting you and your family in India in your journey to better health

Our mission is simple yet profound: to bridge the gap between you and your aging parents in India by offering top-quality healthcare services and support. We aim to alleviate the stress and uncertainty that often comes with long-distance caregiving, allowing you to have peace of mind while ensuring the best possible care for your family.

Building a team of care professionals and using technology to deliver
peace of mind

At Anara, our core belief is that peace of mind should be an integral part of every family's healthcare journey. To fulfill this commitment, we've assembled a dedicated team of compassionate care professionals who understand the unique needs of NRIs caring for their parents in India. Our team not only possesses the expertise necessary to provide exceptional healthcare but also shares the heartfelt dedication to ensuring the well-being of your loved ones.

Moreover, we harness cutting-edge technology as a powerful tool to bridge distances, enabling seamless communication and monitoring of your parents' health. By combining the human touch of our caregivers with the efficiency of modern technology, we empower you with constant visibility into your parents' healthcare, eliminating uncertainty and providing the peace of mind you deserve. Your trust is our highest priority, and we are committed to delivering it through a harmonious blend of compassionate care and innovative solutions. With Anara, rest assured that your parents' health and well-being are in the most capable and caring hands.

Partnering with top hospitals
and providers

At Anara, we are committed to delivering not only the highest quality of healthcare but also ensuring affordability and accessibility for our valued customers. That's why we are excited to offer a best price guarantee through our strategic partnership with Apollo Hospitals, a name synonymous with excellence in healthcare. This collaboration allows us to leverage Apollo's extensive network, world-class medical expertise, and state-of-the-art facilities to provide our customers with top-notch healthcare services at reduced cost. Through this partnership, we aim to make superior healthcare within reach for everyone we serve. It's a step forward in our mission to prioritize both the health and financial well-being of our customers, reaffirming our commitment to delivering better healthcare, hand in hand with affordability.
