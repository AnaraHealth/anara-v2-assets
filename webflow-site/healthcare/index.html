FAQ

Questions families ask first.

Which cities do you serve?
Anara currently supports families in selected Indian cities, including Chennai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chandigarh, and Hyderabad. Coverage can change, so please confirm your parent’s postcode with our team.
How will I receive updates from abroad?
Your care manager agrees a communication rhythm with you and shares clear updates after visits, appointments, and important changes. Urgent issues are escalated using the contacts agreed in the care plan.
What happens in an emergency?
The care plan records key contacts, preferred hospitals, medicines, and practical response steps. Anara helps coordinate the agreed actions, while emergency and medical services remain responsible for clinical decisions and treatment.
Does Anara make medical decisions for my parent?
No. Medical decisions remain with your parent and their qualified clinicians. Anara helps prepare, coordinate, document, and follow up so the plan is easier to carry out.
Can we start with a smaller plan?
Yes. The initial consultation is designed to identify the lightest level of support that can work safely and reliably for your family.
