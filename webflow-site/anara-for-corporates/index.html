Frequently Asked Questions

How does Anara differ from increasing our insurance coverage?
Anara focuses on preventive care and day-to-day health management, which are often not emphasized with traditional insurance. This proactive approach leads to fewer costly health events and healthier employees.
What's the minimum contract period?
We offer flexible contracts, including on-site clinics and a 6-month pilot program for new clients to experience the benefits before full commitment.
How do you ensure data privacy?
We adhere to strict data protection protocols compliant with Indian regulations. Employee health data is never shared with employers; only aggregated, anonymized insights are provided for workforce health management.
Can we customize the services for our company?
Absolutely. We offer a modular approach, allowing you to select services that best fit your workforce needs and company culture.
How does Anara ensure we're getting the best prices for top-quality care?
Our partnerships with Apollo Hospitals and other premium providers include negotiated rates for our clients. We also have a dedicated team that reviews medical recommendations to help prevent unnecessary procedures, for cost-effective, high-quality care.
Can employees choose their own doctors outside of Anara's network?
Yes, our concierge health services and insurance plans offer flexibility. However, we encourage using our network for the best combination of quality and cost-effectiveness. Our concierge team can also help coordinate care with external providers when necessary.
How does Anara's solution compare cost-wise to traditional corporate health plans?
While providing access to premium care, our integrated approach often results in overall cost savings compared to traditional plans, due to our negotiated rates, focus on prevention, and avoidance of unnecessary procedures.
