Privacy Policy

Anara Health Private Limited ("Anara", "we", "us") provides corporate healthcare services to organisations in India. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to (i) employees and family members enrolled in an Anara programme by their employer, (ii) prospective clients who submit enquiries via this website, and (iii) anyone who corresponds with us by email, phone, or WhatsApp.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Enquiry data — name, work email, company, employee count, and any free-text message submitted via our contact form.
  • Clinical data — for enrolled employees: vitals, history, prescriptions, consultation notes, lab results, and care-plan correspondence. Collected only after explicit consent and under medical confidentiality.
  • Operational data — communication metadata (e.g., WhatsApp message timestamps), appointment scheduling, and aggregate utilisation statistics shared with the employer in anonymised form.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected through privacy-respecting analytics.

3. Why we process this data

  • To deliver the healthcare services your employer has contracted us to provide.
  • To respond to enquiries and propose appropriate programmes.
  • To produce anonymised, aggregate reports to the contracting employer (your HR / People function never sees individual records).
  • To comply with applicable Indian medical, tax, and corporate regulations.

4. Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of (i) your explicit consent, obtained at enrolment, (ii) the contract between Anara and your employer, and (iii) legitimate medical interest in the case of urgent care.

5. Data storage and security

Clinical and personal data is stored on infrastructure hosted in Microsoft Azure Central India region. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256; data in transit uses TLS 1.2+. Access is restricted to authorised Anara clinical and operational personnel on a strict need-to-know basis.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with: (a) the contracting employer in anonymised, aggregate form; (b) Anara's clinical network when a referral is required, with your consent; (c) regulators, when compelled by law.

7. Retention

Clinical records are retained for the minimum period required by Indian medical regulations (currently 3 years post-engagement for adult records, longer for paediatric). Enquiry data is retained for 24 months unless you ask us to delete it earlier.

8. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate or out-of-date data.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete data no longer needed for the contracted service.
  • Grievance — escalate any concern to our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Nominate — designate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf.

9. Contact

For any privacy matter — including DPDP rights, breach reports, or grievances — write to privacy@anara.health. Our Grievance Officer details are available on request.

10. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to enrolled employees and posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
